# JSA — JavaScript Analytics

> JSA is a data analytics platform for exploring datasets, preparing data, performing analysis, and presenting results through visualizations and dashboards. Bring a CSV or JSON dataset, inspect and prepare it, analyze it, and present the results as charts, KPIs, and dashboards — all in one focused workspace.
